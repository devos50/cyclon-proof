\paragraph{Scope.} The protocol and stationary law are those of
\texttt{generic-proof.tex}: fixed membership, independent uniform global
initiators, mandatory partner removal, protected incoming identities, and
minimum-age duplicate merging. Empty and underfull caches are allowed.
This companion lower bound has passed independent internal mathematical
review, not formal verification or external peer review.
\section{An identity-only coupon lower bound for every capacity and shuffle length}
Let $n\ge2$, $1\le L\le c\le n-1$, and let $\pi$ be the stationary law
from the generic convergence theorem. Write $\pi_{\rm id}$ for its projection
onto cache identities, forgetting ages. If $d_{\rm id}(t)$ denotes worst-case
variation distance of that projection at global event $t$, then
\[
 d_{\rm id}(t)\ge
 \left[1-\frac{2c+4}{(n-1)(1-1/n)^t}\right]_+.
\]
In particular, whenever the logarithm is positive, reaching error at most
$0<\varepsilon<1$ requires
\[
 t\ge
 \frac{\log\!\left((n-1)(1-\varepsilon)/(2c+4)\right)}
 {-\log(1-1/n)}.
\]
This is $\Omega(n\log(n/c))$ when $c=o(n)$, at fixed error, and
$\Omega(n\log n)$ for fixed capacity. It also lower-bounds full-state
mixing. Unlike an age-survival argument, the witness uses only age-zero
initial descriptors and tests only cache identities. It is not a lower
bound for one fixed peer's single sampled identifier.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Fix $q$. Initially its cache is empty and every $p\ne q$ has singleton cache
$\{q^0\}$. This is an admissible weakly connected state for every allowed
$c,L$. Before the first initiation by $p\ne q$, the identifier $p$ cannot
appear in any other cache: it is absent initially, copying cannot create
it, and its first possible introduction is the fresh self-descriptor in
$p$'s own request. Consequently no other peer can contact $p$ before this
first initiation, so its singleton cache remains unchanged.

Let $U_t$ count outside peers that have never initiated by time $t$, and
let $I_q(t)$ count caches containing $q$. Then $I_q(t)\ge U_t$ pathwise.
Independent uniform initiators give
\[
 \mu_t:=\mathbb EU_t=(n-1)(1-1/n)^t,
 \qquad \operatorname{Var}(U_t)\le\mu_t.
\]
The variance bound follows because distinct non-initiation indicators have
nonpositive covariance:
$(1-2/n)^t\le(1-1/n)^{2t}$ (with the time-zero case read separately).
Stationary label exchangeability and total capacity give
$\mathbb E_\pi I_q=\mathbb E_\pi(\sum_j I_j)/n\le c$.
Chebyshev's inequality therefore yields
$\Pr(I_q(t)\ge\mu_t/2)\ge1-4/\mu_t$, whereas Markov's inequality gives
$\pi(I_q\ge\mu_t/2)\le2c/\mu_t$. Subtracting probabilities of this
identity-measurable event proves the displayed bound and its rearrangement.
